A turnkey vMix multi-cam room on symmetrical fiber.

The control room overlooks the stage through a window and switches up to eight 4K cameras or sixteen 1080p. Redundant workstations, OBS and LiveU encoders, and a Behringer Wing board are already patched. The system can be configured to your camera and audio plan, and camera and support packages are available on request.
