A clean stage, every call time.

We take the health and safety of every client, collaborator and crew member seriously, and follow current state, local and motion-picture-industry guidelines. The stage you walk onto was cleaned before your call.

Standards on every shoot day.

These are in place for every booking, from a two-person podcast to a multi-cam Technocrane build. Ask the studio manager on the day if your production needs anything beyond them.

  • Both stages and all ancillary rooms disinfected daily
  • High-touch areas sanitized throughout the day
  • Hand-sanitizing stations on every stage
  • PPE and supplies available on request
  • Safety protocols posted on all stages
  • Daily wellness checks for all Agency Studios staff
  • ADA-compliant restrooms and a private dressing room
  • Facility monitored 24/7 by interior and exterior cameras

Secured property, day and night.

The parking lot is lit, secured, and locked each night, and the main gate closes automatically after hours. Crew and talent have a covered outdoor lounge away from the stage floor.

  1. Secured lot

    Lit and CCTV-monitored 24/7, with two entrances so trucks never back out onto Duarte Road.

  2. Controlled entry

    Executive-office doors use HID card and fingerprint access. Guest badges are issued for your crew list.

  3. Camera-ready stairs

    Mezzanine stairs are carpeted and lit with flicker-free LEDs, safe for gear and for shooting on.

  4. Rainy-day load-in

    One of the two loading bays sits under an awning, so cases stay dry from the truck to the stage.
