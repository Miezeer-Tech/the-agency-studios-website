What a stage rental covers

  1. Space and equipment

    A full stage rental includes the amenities for crew and performers. Customize with backgrounds, lighting packages, and specialized equipment as needed.

  2. Production support

    Add PA systems, radios, and displays. Daily cleaning and extra parking can be arranged for the run of the shoot.

  3. Comfort

    Makeup mirrors, wardrobe racks, and talent amenities. Director's chairs and pop-up tents for the lot.

  4. Custom packages

    Discounted grip and lighting packages are built for your production. Camera packages and the control room are quoted per shoot.
