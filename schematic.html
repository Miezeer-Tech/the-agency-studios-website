Studio schematic

Walk the floor before you load in.

Tap any marker on the plan to see what's there. Scroll or pinch to zoom, drag to pan. North is to the left of the drawing.

100%
  1. Stage 13,000 sq ft main stage, 60′ × 53′

    The main stage. Overall studio space of 3,000 sq ft with a 20′ ceiling to the lighting grid. This is where the Technocrane builds happen.

  2. Two-wall cyc2,200 sq ft, 47′ × 43′

    A seamless two-wall cyclorama: a 47′ wall and a 43′ cyc on a 45′ wall. Drape it black for set builds or shoot it clean.

  3. Stage 2, insert stage1,128 sq ft, 24′ × 47′

    One-wall cyc and open space. Painted green for keying, and it runs at the same time as Stage 1 for double-up days.

  4. Two elephant doorsDrive-in load-in from the side lot

    Two load-in elephant doors on the lot side of Stage 1. Trucks pull right up; grip and set pieces roll straight onto the stage.

  5. Company switch600 A, 3-phase

    Cam-lock tie-in at the company switch between the stages. Run your distro without a generator.

  6. Multi-use roomDIT, audio, lighting

    A closed room off the stage for the DIT station, sound cart, or lighting board, out of the shot and out of the noise.

  7. Green rooms 01 and 02Wardrobe and makeup

    Two green rooms off Stage 2 for wardrobe and hair-and-makeup, with lit mirrors and talent seating.

  8. KitchenetteCrafty and client hold

    A kitchenette beside the green rooms for craft services and coffee between setups.

  9. Talent bathroom and showerPlus a separate crew bathroom

    A talent makeup bathroom with a private shower, and a separate crew bathroom, all off the stage corridor.

  10. StorageCases and stands

    Storage for cases, stands, and expendables so the stage floor stays clear.

  11. Corporate officesNo production access

    The Agency Advertising Group's private offices share the building. No production access, and no production parking in front of them.

  12. Production parkingSide lot, by the elephant doors

    All production parking is in the side lot, where trucks and crew cars line up next to the elephant doors.

Dimensions are from the studio's own spec sheet. Bring a tape for anything that has to fit to the inch, and tell us in the booking form what you're bringing in.
