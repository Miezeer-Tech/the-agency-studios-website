Book the Podcast RoomHow it works

Four steps from booking to episode.

You bring the conversation. The room, the crew position, and the signal chain are already set when you walk in.

  1. Book your slot

    Pick a date, how many people are on mic, and whether you're recording or going live. We confirm the rate and the time within the day.

  2. Walk in, sit down

    The set is dressed, the mics are on the arms, and three cameras are framed on the sofa and chairs. Guests use the green room next door.

  3. We run the room

    Levels, camera switching, and on-screen graphics are handled from the RØDECaster. Ask for a light change between segments and it happens on cue.

  4. Leave with the show

    Take the multi-cam switch or the isolated feeds on a drive. Going live? The stream goes out on the studio's fiber while you record.

Everything on the rate card, nothing hidden.

The hourly rate covers the room, the gear, and an operator. Light changes and live broadcast are on the request form so we can staff for them.

  • Up to 4 Shure SM7B podcast mics on boom arms

  • Live RØDECaster switcher with an operator
  • On-screen display for guests, names, and graphics
  • Light-change setup between segments
