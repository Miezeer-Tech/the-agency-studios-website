Safe set

A clean stage, every call time.

We take the health and safety of every client, collaborator, and crew seriously, and follow current state, local, and motion-picture-industry guidelines.

  • Both stages and all ancillary rooms are disinfected daily
  • High-touch areas sanitized throughout the day
  • Hand-sanitizing stations on every stage
  • PPE and supplies available on request
  • Safety protocols posted on all stages
  • Daily wellness checks for all Agency Studios staff
